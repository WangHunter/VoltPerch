VoltPerch application icon

A mint bird on a single perch, with a lightning-shaped cutout for the wing.
A dark petrol tile frames the mark on light and dark Windows surfaces. At
small sizes, the rim, cutout and perch are strengthened to preserve the
silhouette. There are no decorative details, shadows or gradients.

Source: tools/IconBuilder/Program.cs. From the repository root, regenerate
app.ico and the 256 px app-preview.png with:

    dotnet run --project tools/IconBuilder -c Release

Windows frames: 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px, with transparent
corners. The notification-area icon remains a separate, live battery gauge.
